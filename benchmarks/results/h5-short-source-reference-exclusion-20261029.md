## Scope of the exclusion

The raw runner and response reader deliberately make no original-source
infeasibility claim. The separate [necessity qualification](h5-short-source-reference-exclusion-20261029/source-necessity-audit.json)
checks 4,883 recursive input bindings and the full chain from original geometry

source-contained cores and strips, stock domains, rounding, legal mode choices,
identical-copy relabeling, arbitrary independent real cog angles, original cog

source layout of the target allocation would satisfy the complete model.
Consequently its terminal infeasibility excludes that allocation.

The [independent frontier audit](h5-short-source-reference-exclusion-20261029/frontier-audit.json) compares every row
and verifies that only 5/6/2, 5/7/2 and 5/8/2 acquire the new exclusion label.
Prior labels and first-sheet complements remain intact. The valid 64-part source
layout is preserved byte for byte. No new source-valid 13-part or 66-part
placement, whole-job infeasibility, production improvement or H5 completion
follows from this result.
