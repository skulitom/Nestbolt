and N5/H6 now contribute **45%** of the v1.1 milestone weights. H2, H4 and H5
remain open. This is correctness and preservation evidence; it is not a new
published v1 quality run or a release.

The tested product is `5fb6b5a6620ae63c4d4fb431f2c235c7ea547056`. The evaluator,
training calibration and [reserved-project plan](h3-heldout-20261015/plan.json)

The [machine audit](h3-complete-20261015/checks.json) verifies the frozen plan,
binary/source guards, result coverage and all 1,181 frozen inventory hashes.

## Reserved-project results

The [three retained outcomes](h3-heldout-20261015/results.json) all pass. The
training-only [calibration](h3-evaluator-calibration-20261015T205719Z/results.json)
first passed 65 leaves / 81 contours on `lb-02`. It is not counted again below.

Each project has three movable rigid units and one fixed shape. All three
membership/hole/layer comparisons are exact. The nine canonical comparisons
pass. Maximum bounding-box difference is **0.000499232 mm**, image-corner
difference **0.000000729 mm**, and public collision-footprint boundary difference
**0.003717625 mm**. Both independent placement overlap and outside areas are zero.
Two omitted-zero-handle shapes in `lb-01` also match its paired native SVG;
that reference check was declared in the frozen plan.

Every actual CLI search used the same predeclared 2000 × 2000 mm stock, 2 mm
spacing/edge spacing, default quarter-turn rotations without mirroring, three
repeatable seconds, seed 0 and a 90-second engine deadline. Commands ran
sequentially on physical CCD0 CPU 8 and returned three units on one sheet.
This generous common stock tests preservation and validity, not packing quality.
Native standalone validation agrees exactly with the saved metrics.

The three projects are now **evaluation-only**. They and their related reserved
inputs remain excluded from product or parameter tuning. The original split,
hashes and labels are unchanged. The exclusive `opened.json` marker remains;
the evaluator refuses an automatic repeat.

## Current native replay and retained limitations

Revalidation used the five already saved native-checked layouts, with no new
searches and only training/authored inputs. Two regenerated outputs are byte
identical. The other three differ only in line endings and top-level numeric
replay, at most **2.274e-13 mm**; every other canonical element is exact. This
retains the original native observations without claiming another desktop run.

All eleven original public-import rejections remain visible: six require cached
movable Text, one needs active/fixed artwork regrouped in LightBurn, and four
contain no movable unit. Two otherwise importable originals also have fixed
uncached Text warnings; their complete geometry checks use their prepared copies.
Rejecting mixed groups preserves both the rigid-group and fixed-shape contracts.

Native repair is not a lossless save of the original project. In particular,
L42's native Scan+Cut-to-Scan change still fails the original cutting-layer
comparison; its repaired file's own XML/settings are preserved exactly. It is
not relabelled as an original-layer match. The earlier geometry failures,
search failures, oracle accommodations, diagnostic assertions and native
normalization findings remain in the prior reports and archives.
