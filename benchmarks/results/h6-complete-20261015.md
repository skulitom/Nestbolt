## Material-cost method

Product build `77dfa55ceabc7680ce5d332dc7682bc25f6b5ab6` ran on one physical CCD0 core per process, at most eight concurrently. Each arm had a 60 s wall-clock search budget, seeds 0–2. Both arms used common-line mode with its search bonus off, at identical 0.2 mm laser or 1.5 mm plasma kerf spacing. Only default line allowances differed: 3 mm, 45° outside and 90° in holes. Stock, quantities, rotations, mirrors and margins were unchanged.

The [frozen set](../datasets/cutting/manifest.json) contains the eight maker jobs named in H5 and five authored rectangle/bracket jobs. Original polygon rings are represented as exact lines, with no new simplification. Each public derived job must equal the source job except the requested kerf clearance. No held-out geometry was read.

Material is the independent normalized stock-consumption metric, including the extents of actual reserved leads. Each job/kerf comparison uses `(median lead material / median baseline material − 1) × 100`; the headline is the median of the 13 job differences. This measures the complete equal-budget search workflow, not the geometric area of the leads alone. H6 requires the cost to be reported and imposes no maximum cost.

## Report-order failure retained and resolved

The raw harness fully scored 155/156 runs. Both geometry validations of the remaining mixed-rectangle/plasma baseline passed, and its material metrics matched. An f64 JSON round trip moved one minimum-X coordinate by about 1e-14 mm, swapping two entries in the unordered COMMON edge/path arrays. The position-by-position report comparison rejected them.

The comparator now pairs those two unordered collections using stable keys, then checks the unrounded fields at the original 1e-9 absolute tolerance. It keeps every segment order, owner order, field and duplicate count. A regression rejects a 1e-7 coordinate change, added paths and changed owners. This corrects report comparison; it does not change geometry, a gate threshold or a frozen label.

All 156 archives and their internal hashes were checked. Every saved original/revalidated report matches under the corrected comparison; the largest numeric difference is 2.9103830456733704e-11. The one affected saved layout was freshly revalidated on CPU0, with unchanged source and binary hashes and no new search. The original failed result, traceback, layout, exports and runner remain preserved. [Recheck and correction](h6-material-recheck-20261015/reconciled.json).

The earlier two-/five-second pilot failures and the owner-fit performance fix remain in the [pilot checkpoint](h6-material-pilot-checkpoint-20261015.md). The full run’s longest nest/export wall time was 79.09 s, including work outside the 60 s search budget; no process reached its external limit. This is not a general speed claim.
