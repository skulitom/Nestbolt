Nestbolt comparisons

We make Nestbolt. Three pages compare it with other nesting tools. The Deepnest alternative page gives the material use measured in Nestbolt's frozen benchmark against Deepnest, SVGnest and Sparrow, with the date of the run. The nesting for AI agents page lists which nesting tools an AI agent can call through an MCP server, a command line or a library; it says which numbers we measured and which we read from the other tools' own pages, and when it was last checked. The LightBurn page sets out the nesting options for LightBurn users without Pro, from LightBurn's own docs, beta notes, forum and early-access page, with a measured run of Nestbolt's LightBurn preview.

A Deepnest alternative, with the benchmark to inspect

Checked 2026-11-22. In Nestbolt's frozen v1 benchmark (build 75cbb32, final acceptance run of 2026-10-13), Nestbolt used 8.5% less material at the median than the best of Deepnest, SVGnest and Sparrow at equal 60-second budgets, and 9.6% at 300 seconds on the long subset. Sparrow, which Nestbolt builds on, still wins 8 of the 56 jobs at 60 seconds. The Deepnest in that run was deepnest-next as of 6 October 2026, not the 1.6.0-beta.0 and 1.6.0-beta.1 pre-releases of 20 and 21 November 2026, which we have not run. The page explains how the benchmark was run, what changed in those two pre-releases, when to choose Deepnest instead and where Sparrow wins. The results page has every per-job number.

Nestbolt's DXF import, checked 2026-11-27. On 27 drawings we made of the same three plates, the command-line build of Nestbolt's main branch (commit 4ac32f7) passed 12 of 12 checks, on the $INSUNITS units with codes 1 to 21, binary DXF, a frozen and a switched-off layer, and 38 plates placed on three sheets and counted again in the exported DXF. The v0.2.0-alpha.1 download passed 7 of the 12 and stable v0.1.0 passed 6; neither download has the DXF import changes merged on 24 November 2026 yet. Only Nestbolt's command line was run on these files; we did not test the hosted app. Fixtures, script and results.

Nesting for AI agents: Nestbolt, OpenNest, pyFit, Sparrow and Deepnest compared

Checked 2026-11-22. Reading the tools' repositories, three MCP directories and GitHub search, we found three open-source nesting tools that an AI agent can call over MCP, the same three as on 2026-10-20: Nestbolt (5 tools, SVG and DXF in), OpenNest by ajisaacs (19 tools in its v0.7.0 source and 33 on its master branch; DXF, DWG or SVG in; built from source) and pyFit (4 tools). SmartCut, CutOptim and NestSolver are hosted MCP services that nest true shapes, and the desktop program nestpro, which has an MCP server and a command line, publishes its source under a small-business licence, not an open-source one. Sparrow, CLI-nesting and sheetnest also have command lines, while Deepnest and SVGnest have none. We ran only Nestbolt: three fresh v0.1.0 MCP servers nested its example job six times, placing 32 of 32 parts on one sheet each time, and all six layouts were byte-identical, to each other and to the six from 2026-10-20. Everything about the other tools comes from their own documentation.

LightBurn nesting without Pro, including onto offcuts: True Nest, Quick Nest and Nestbolt compared

Checked 2026-11-21. In LightBurn 2.2, True Nest needs a Pro licence ($199, or a $100 upgrade from Core, the upgrade price as read on 2026-11-05); Core has Quick Nest, which packs bounding rectangles, and SVGNest (External), which uses the SVGnest.com website. LightBurn 2.3, in public beta since 11 November 2026, adds Full Nest, which nests around shapes locked inside the container (LightBurn's lead developer, 18 November 2026); LightBurn's early-access page says some new features require Pro without naming them, and we found no release date for 2.3. We ran Nestbolt's free v0.2.0-alpha.1 preview on a three-layer LightBurn project of engraved ornaments: 18 on one 12 × 12 inch sheet, 13 on a traced offcut and 11 on a partly used sheet with two holes, each written back as an editable .lbrn2 with every layer kept, checked by our own script. LightBurn was not run; its facts come from its own docs, beta notes, early-access page, forum and feature-request board.

The hosted browser app runs a preview build from main; stable v0.1.0 remains a download.
