Nestbolt / Examples / Interlocking gears

Nest gears with free rotation

This drawing requests 40 gears in two sizes on a 300 × 200 mm bed. Free rotation lets their teeth mesh in the arrangement, making it a useful example of a job where orientations are not limited to quarter turns.

Open Nestbolt in your browser

Free, MIT-licensed, no account; your files never leave your computer.

What this job asks for

There are two part types, with 20 copies of each. The request uses a 5 mm sheet margin, 1 mm part spacing and 1 mm edge spacing, with a 20-second search budget. Free rotation is the permitted orientation rule for both types.

Bundled example settings
Sheet (mm) 300 × 200
Margin 5 mm
Part spacing 1 mm
Edge spacing 1 mm
Allowed rotations Free rotation
Part types and copies 2 types; 40 copies: gear-1 × 20, gear-2 × 20
Time budget 20 seconds

Measured layout

Measured on 2026-10-20 with the build this site hosts (main at c9b0176), in a headless Chromium browser on a desktop PC, with the example’s own settings: the 20-second search placed 40 of 40 gears on one sheet, 20 of them inside the holes of the larger gears, with 32.6% utilisation and material 0.976 sheets, leaving a 300 × 11 mm strip. The independent check: valid. Results vary a little between runs and computers, because the search stops at its time limit.

Try it yourself

Open the app, click the button “Try an example: interlocking gears”; it loads the drawing and starts nesting straight away. Or download the gear drawing and drop it in, applying the settings above.

Watch the live best layout to see the orientations the search chooses. The independent validator checks overlap, spacing, containment, rotations and quantities before a layout is returned. Export each sheet as SVG or DXF from the original curves, then review it in your cutting workflow.

Use your own file

  • Choose free rotation only when it is permitted for your job. Compare this example with the sewing pattern's restricted 0°/180° list.
  • Keep the quantities explicit for each size; this request uses 20 of each rather than a single total count.
  • Review part spacing as well as the bed margin. Free rotation does not remove the request's spacing constraints.

The example demonstrates packing gear outlines. Nestbolt gives no machine-safety or fit certification, so the resulting arrangement does not certify the gears' mechanical fit.
