Nestbolt results

Development results: three seeds per job, held-out jobs excluded. These are not the final acceptance run, which uses fresh seeds 1000-1004 and adds ten held-out jobs.

Material used = (sheets − 1) + used length of the last sheet ÷ its length, from an independent Clipper2 validator; lower is better. Each tool runs single-threaded at equal wall-clock budgets on the same PC, at most eight runs at once. A competitor's value per job is the better of its two settings (identical constraints, its own best settings); jobs where no competitor produced a valid layout earn no wins. G2 passes when the median reduction against the best competitor is at least 5.00%, Nestbolt wins or ties at least 90% of jobs and is never more than 1% worse (plan, section 6; the earlier raise to 10.77% was waived on 2026-10-12, decision 4).
